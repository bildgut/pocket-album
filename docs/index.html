Your albums and photos from your own Immich server, quick to open, easy to find and still there when you're offline. Pocket Album is a small native iPhone and iPad app written in Swift and SwiftUI.

Join the beta Latest release Source code

What it does

  • Photos or albums first: opens on your photos by default, or switch to albums first in Settings
  • 30-day swipe deck: quickly review the last 30 days in a card stack to mark favorites or collect shots for the trash
  • Deliberately offline: keep albums, favorites, recent days (3–30 days) or chosen people on your device as previews or originals, refreshed in the background
  • Explore: find photos across your library and shared albums by people, places and years, or just type what you're looking for
  • Photo info & TV slideshow: inspect camera, lens and map details in the viewer, or run a full-screen slideshow via AirPlay with the phone as the remote
  • No upload, no backup: talks only to your server and works fine with a read-only API key

What you need

  • An iPhone or iPad with iOS 26 or later
  • An Immich server, version 3.2 or later
  • An API key; the README lists the permissions
